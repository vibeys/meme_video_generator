VIDEO TITLE: The Baby Monitor Secret

NARRATION TEXT (paste this into the post-text prompt):

After our daughter was born, my husband became the most helpful partner I could ask for. One afternoon, he went upstairs when the baby monitor crackled. I heard him comfort our little girl, then say she would soon have someone else to call Mom.

I confronted him the next day. He told me that years ago, when we were dating, he had been seriously ill. His recovery was not a miracle, he said. He had made a bargain with something strange, and our daughter was part of the price.

Before I could ask anything else, laughter came through the monitor from the nursery. My husband begged me not to go upstairs. He said they would leave us alone if our next child was a boy.

That was when I understood: whatever was in the nursery had not come to help our family.
